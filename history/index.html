USDe Risk Audit / History

Earlier USDe research, preserved in context

A record of the previous programme—not a second current answer and not a new source of live settings.

Historical material—not the current assessment. This collection preserves the previous programme as it stood in the 14 September 2026 release. Its five reports are dated 11–13 September; their observations and source limits keep their own dates. For the current whole-system judgement, use the assessment or the 18 September foundation.

Preserving a report means preserving what it said and what it could establish at the time. It does not endorse every historical number as current, import its tests into a new investigation, or turn a previous proposal into an installed safeguard. The older programme’s numbering remains part of its own history.

Two date traps to avoid

The historical control snapshot is 11 September 2026. The earlier control and staking discussions use Ethereum block 25,952,841 at 08:03:35 UTC. Reading those pages today does not refresh their threshold, cap, cooldown or missing-control conclusions. The new foundation identifies its own later observed bindings and distinct remaining limitations.

The older backing arithmetic is November 2025. The first historical report’s approximately 101% arithmetic uses an issuer disclosure for 26 November 2025, 23:59 UTC. It was not an attestation performed by that report, did not include an inspection of the embedded letters, and was not a September 2026 reserve statement. It is not carried into the current assessment as current coverage. Read that historical calculation in its original context.

What remains unchanged—and how to navigate it

The dated reports remain as originally published, including their old navigation and source links. Their overview, research, scope or method links can therefore lead to the current versions now occupying those routes. Use the dated context links above when the earlier surrounding pages are needed. Historical labels and this explanation are in the current indexes and navigation, not inserted into those report bodies.

The current investigation did not inherit the earlier programme’s complete Mint V2 source bundle, model-test results or all historical assurance reading as newly accepted research. Differences in source access or inspection strength are not by themselves evidence that a deployment changed or that an older conclusion was disproved. The current method keeps those evidence boundaries explicit.

The old diagrams and curated calculation records remain part of their dated reports, at their original asset routes. They are historical exhibits rather than live widgets or fresh balances. For the new cases and their exact observation dates, return to the whole-system foundation.

This pool studies how USDe-dependent positions can lose value or access to it. Its conclusions are bounded by the evidence, not a security certification, reserve assurance opinion or personal investment recommendation.
