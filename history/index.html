The dates that most easily get confused

Control state: the older supplied Ethereum snapshot is block 25,952,841, 11 September 2026, 08:03:35 UTC. Newer state observations do not change what was or was not established at that older block. Later core observations.

Backing arithmetic: the first historical report’s approximately 101% calculation uses the issuer’s 26 November 2025, 23:59 UTC disclosure. It was an arithmetic check, not inspected underlying custody letters or a September 2026 reserve statement. Read the historical calculation in context.

Source inspection: an earlier source-model test or provider bundle is not a fresh transaction, independent deployment rebuild or proof of current recovery. The 18 September assessment identifies its own source and observation limits. Evidence trail.
