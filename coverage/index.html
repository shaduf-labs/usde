USDe / audit scope

Which evidence do we actually have?

A custody balance, a release permission and a completed payment answer different questions.

This view includes research 5: custody and usable funds. Eighteen discovered components remain visible; this is not a percentage of the whole ecosystem. The earlier Ethereum snapshot is still dated 11 September 2026.

New operational detail, not new account verification. Provider documents describe pending settlement and release conditions. No private account, current balance sheet, signed customer agreement or matching live settlement receipt was inspected.

Discovery, source reading and current verification remain separate.
