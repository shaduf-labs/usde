USDe / audit scope

Evidence has edges.

A source file, a matched deployment, a configured permission and a completed payment answer different questions.

Updated through research 4: direct redemption. Prior source investigations remain useful; their historical observations are not relabelled current. The table keeps all eighteen discovered components visible, not a percentage of the entire ecosystem.

Provider corroboration is not independent compilation. No new common-block state, live redemption receipt, market quote or current backing reconciliation was obtained in this update.

Source scope and missing evidence are separate. Unknown does not mean low risk.
