What this assessment covers—and what it does not

The evidence connects the system while leaving specific recovery, execution and security questions unresolved.

Analysis dated 1 October 2026 · No new on-chain observations · Financial inputs retain their 27–28 September dates · Financed-recovery and deployed-control coverage remain partial

This assessment follows how USDe-dependent positions can lose value or access. It distinguishes the selected source and deployed-state checks from unverified recovery and execution behavior. It is not complete security, solvency or exit assurance.

What stronger evidence should resolve

For the integration result, the missing joins are now narrower: complete Base-position prior financing and sufficient subsequent proceeds; the intended measured sUSDe/USDC or sUSDe/USDT account; the PT’s final owed-token conversion; and shareholder-specific mobilization of the screened vault resources. Reconciled evidence gaps and their consequences.

The recovery investigation makes the selected chains concrete but leaves decisive joins open: who legally controls the observed holding, what exact notice or redemption conditions apply, what net proceeds are claimable, and when those proceeds reach the paying entity. Actual borrower payments, holder mandates and matched fund-to-issuer transfers would narrow these questions more than another headline portfolio percentage. Specific evidence that would change the result.

Deployed security and control semantics then need to be tied to consequential operations, particularly Mint V2 and the Base meta-oracle mismatch. Funded integration unwinds need actual debt assets, collateral realisation, bridges or remote conversion capacity, and the first party bearing residual loss. Joint stress must follow those verified dependencies rather than assume circularity from shared names.

This is a continuing investigation, not a promise of a future completion date or a fixed number of checks that would certify USDe. The foundation’s limitation section names the unresolved conclusions; the method page explains what each evidence type can support.
