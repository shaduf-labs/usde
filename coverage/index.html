USDe Risk Audit / Coverage

What this assessment covers—and what it does not

A connected opening assessment can be complete as an investigation while still leaving material assurance questions unanswered.

Research basis: 18 September 2026 · Research: GPT-6 Pro · Dated evidence, not a live monitor

The opening investigation follows the complete question—how USDe-dependent positions can lose value or access—without claiming every component, exposure or failure path has been audited. “Complete” describes that bounded opening task. It does not mean complete security, solvency or exit assurance.

The largest missing conclusion is a current recoverable-backing assessment. The research did not obtain the latest attestation body, reconcile present assets and liabilities, establish encumbrances and private recovery rights, or measure an asset-specific cash ladder. No current reserve percentage or liquidity sufficiency score is supported. Backing evidence and limitations.

The actual case studies—not the whole ecosystem

Ethereum Aave: the installed direct-sUSDe price path was identified, including its share ratio and capped USDT/USD input. The investigation did not replace that evidence with a proposed principal-token market. Base reserve settings and eMode settings remain separate, as do their 17 September date and the 18 September price reads. Read the case.

Base Morpho and Steakhouse: one USDC/USDe market was bound to its actual oracle and supplying adapter. Concentration is measured against the selected vault’s assets, not Ethena’s reserves. The separate named Ethena-oriented vault showed dust in the tested market, not a demonstrated material circular-funding loop or proof of zero exposure elsewhere. Market and oracle; allocation and ownership boundary.

Ethereum secondary exit: one pool and four independent quote sizes. The small near-par example and weak large-size proceeds belong together. They are not a global depeg measurement and do not establish a Base liquidation route. Quotes, addresses and arguments.

What stronger evidence should resolve

The next substantive priority is the recovery and payment chain: which entity owns each asset, who owes each receivable, what is encumbered, how a recall or sale works, and when the required currency is usable. That would distinguish losses that threaten principal from waits that require liquidity but may still end in full recovery.

Deployed security and control semantics then need to be tied to consequential operations, particularly Mint V2 and the Base meta-oracle mismatch. Funded integration unwinds need actual debt assets, collateral realisation, bridges or remote conversion capacity, and the first party bearing residual loss. Joint stress must follow those verified dependencies rather than assume circularity from shared names.

This is a continuing investigation, not a promise of a future completion date or a fixed number of checks that would certify USDe. The foundation’s limitation section names the unresolved conclusions; the method page explains what each evidence type can support.

Older scope is preserved, not merged

The earlier programme’s scope page described eighteen discovered components and accepted source paths under its own evidence corpus. That count was not an ecosystem coverage percentage and is not a denominator for this investigation. The previous reports’ model-test counts and 11 September control snapshot are not newly executed checks here.

Use the preserved earlier scope to understand those dated reports, and History to reach the complete earlier context. The current assessment rests on the evidence and qualifications in the new whole-system foundation.
