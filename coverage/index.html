USDe / inspection boundary

What was actually checked?

A document read, a source-code trace and a verified live setting are different kinds of evidence.

This first investigation covers the system’s major mechanisms at different depths. It does not supply a complete, exposure-weighted list of every connected market. No overall audit percentage is justified.

Core and staking

Historical token, mint V1, staking, silo, admin and selected ERC-4626 paths were read.

Boundary: Full selected files; imported tree incomplete. No current deployment/configuration match.

Mint V2 and audit history

V2 documentation and historical audit findings were compared with accessible source.

Boundary: Fixes repository unavailable. An audit report is not current remediation proof.

Direct redemption

Public terms and Mint User Agreement were read.

Boundary: No personal eligibility or jurisdiction-specific enforceability conclusion.

Backing and counterparties

Hedge, reserve, custody, credit, real-world asset and non-crypto strategy documents examined.

Boundary: Current positions, private agreements and available cash unverified.

Backing disclosure

November 26, 2025 publisher totals reconciled with their rounding precision.

Boundary: Embedded letters unread; no current assurance or independent balance verification.

Pendle and Strata

Three Pendle adapter/base files; PT/SY/oracle and Strata design documents.

Boundary: Exact live markets/adapters unknown; Strata implementation not audited.

Aave and Morpho

Monad proposal plus later challenge; selected Morpho withdrawal/liquidation source.

Boundary: No proof the proposed market was activated; current collateral, pricing, liquidation limit and interest model unverified.

Market exits and chains

Sale, bridge and cross-chain dependency boundaries identified.

Boundary: No executable depth quote, bridge implementation review or complete chain inventory.

The highest-value missing checks

Match core and staking source to deployments, read their actual permissions and cooldown at a common finalized block, then align backing and custody evidence by time. Exact market identities, oracle settings and sale routes come before liquidity or liquidation claims.

The issuer’s development-Safe documents still conflict; the current threshold is unknown in this report. A proposed Monad PT-USDe market also has an unresolved later availability challenge. address description; architecture description; market proposal and replies.
