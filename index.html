A signed redemption is not a completed exit.

Holding USDe does not automatically establish issuer-redemption access. Even an approved holder's valid signature does not reserve capacity or force an operator to submit. The inspected contract protects a later boundary: a reverting collateral payout rolls back the same transaction's USDe burn.

The earlier steps still matter

The staking investigation explains fixed queued USDe, reward cutoff and the account's common release time. Its controller analysis separates ordinary scheduled execution from the separate whitelist path; current permissions and a universal notice window were not established.

The control snapshot is Ethereum block 25,952,841, 11 September 2026 at 08:03:35 UTC. Its Safe threshold, global caps and one-day cooldown are different, dated observations—not current or protocol-wide promises. The new direct-redemption research does not refresh those settings.
